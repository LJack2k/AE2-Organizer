## Drag & drop

Build filters by dragging items straight onto your filter windows.

- **Drop an item on a tab** - a prompt asks whether to add it **by name** or **by mod**, and adds that condition to the tab.
- **Drop it on the new `+` cell** at the end of the bar - opens the editor with a new tab already filled in: named and iconed after the item, and set up to match just that item. Adjust it and save.
- **Drag from anywhere** - an item on your cursor (from the terminal or your inventory), or straight out of JEI's item list. The item always goes back to your inventory afterwards, so nothing gets dropped on the floor.
- **Right-click a tab** to open the editor on that tab directly.

Works the same on Applied Energistics 2 terminals and Refined Storage grids. Still fully client-side, and your existing tabs and settings carry over untouched.
