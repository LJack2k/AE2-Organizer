### Fixed

- **Minecraft 1.20.1:** AE2 terminals now open correctly. (1.21.1 and 26.1 were not affected.)
- **Dark mode:** button labels are readable again with AE2 dark-mode packs, and switching a pack on mid-session re-themes straight away.
- **GUI scale:** filter windows stay where you put them when the GUI scale changes.
- **JEI sync:** fixed a crash with filters longer than 128 characters. Long filters are now shortened to fit JEI's search box.

### Added

- A warning in the tab editor when a filter is too long to sync with JEI.
